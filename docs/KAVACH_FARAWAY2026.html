The Problem

India has no space weather warning system.

  • 1.4 billion people — served by 28 power utilities, no early warning layer
  • Existing systems are English-only, internet-only — built for the American grid, useless for India's last mile
  • GPS failure hits farmers first — precision agriculture, fishing navigation, all go dark at once
  • ₹3,000 crore in transformer assets at risk every G5 event — with no automated pre-warning
