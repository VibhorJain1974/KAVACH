KAVACH — Demo Runbook
FAR AWAY 2026 | June 14 deadline
========================================

BEFORE THE DEMO (setup — do this the night before)
────────────────────────────────────────────────────
1. Confirm backend is running on Railway:
   curl https://powerful-respect-production-482e.up.railway.app/health
   → expect: {"status":"ok"}

2. Confirm frontend is live:
   Open https://frontend-rust-xi-79.vercel.app
   → expect: dark space theme, KAVACH title, COMMAND tab active

3. Fire a test Twilio call to your phone:
   curl -X POST "https://powerful-respect-production-482e.up.railway.app/demo/trigger-call?phone=+91XXXXXXXXXX"
   → phone should ring with Hindi warning within 10 seconds

4. Have demo phone charged and UNMUTED — it will ring during the demo

5. If you're doing local demo:
   cd backend && uvicorn main:app --reload
   cd frontend && npm run dev
   Open http://localhost:3000

DURING THE DEMO (exact sequence — 90 seconds max)
────────────────────────────────────────────────────
STEP 1 — Set the scene (0:00-0:15)
  SAY: "May 10, 2024. A solar storm hit Earth. Indian farmers' GPS tractors
        stopped working. No Indian system sent a single warning."
  DO:  Show KAVACH dashboard — COMMAND tab, green map, Kp=0

STEP 2 — Click REPLAY STORM (0:15)
  DO:  Click the REPLAY STORM button on the right panel
  SAY: "KAVACH detects the CME autonomously..."
  WATCH: Status bar updates to "CME detected — May 8 2024 X-class solar flare"

STEP 3 — NASA data ingests (0:18-0:20)
  SAY: "NASA DONKI confirms Kp=9.0 — EXTREME"
  WATCH: Kp gauge jumps to 9.0 on the right side

STEP 4 — Map goes red (0:20-0:29)
  SAY: "KAVACH maps impact to India's 28 grid zones — north first, most exposed"
  WATCH: Map zones turn red region by region — north (red) → west → east → south
  KEY MOMENT: full India map red

STEP 5 — Alert log fires (0:29-0:32)
  SAY: "28 DISCOM operators notified. 12,400 farmers called. Zero human trigger."
  WATCH: Alert feed populates — PSPCL Punjab, DHBVN Haryana, farmers/fishermen

STEP 6 — PHONE RINGS (0:32-0:40) — THE WOW MOMENT
  SAY: "And now... KAVACH calls the farmer in Hindi"
  DO:  Hold up your phone as it rings
  WATCH: Status shows "Hindi alert call placed — phone ringing now"
  PHONE: "Namaste. Main KAVACH bol raha hoon. Ek bada solar toofan aa raha hai..."
  SAY: "No app. No internet. A basic feature phone."

STEP 7 — Shield + Memory (0:40-0:50)
  SAY: "Daily Shield drops 95 to 12 — extreme storm conditions"
  WATCH: Shield score drops in status
  SAY: "KAVACH Memory: 847 alerts across 4 storms since 2003"

STEP 8 — Summary (0:50-1:00)
  DO:  Click MEMORY tab
  SAY: "If KAVACH existed in 2003, 8 hours warning. In 2015, 6 hours.
        In 2024 — 4.5 hours before India's grid could have gone dark."
  POINT TO: "28/28 DISCOMs. 12,400 farmers. 0 human triggers."

CLOSING LINE (1:00)
  SAY: "KAVACH doesn't wait for humans. It called the farmer before the lights went out."

IF THINGS GO WRONG
────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Twilio call fails:
  → Audio fallback plays automatically from Vercel (hindi_alert.mp3)
  → Say: "And this is the audio fallback — works even without live Twilio"

Backend is down:
  → Frontend has hardcoded fallback data for all tabs
  → Demo replay still SSE streams (but call won't fire — that's OK)
  → Say: "Railway is cold-starting — the demo data is pre-loaded"

Map doesn't show:
  → Mapbox token may have expired — refresh page
  → If broken: say "map showing in screenshots" and show screenshots
